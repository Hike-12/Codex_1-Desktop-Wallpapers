Release builds bundle FFmpeg and ffprobe from FFmpeg 8.1.1, built without
GPL or nonfree options. FFmpeg is copyright the FFmpeg developers and is
distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.
wallp runs these programs as separate processes. You can replace them by
setting WALLP_FFMPEG_DIR to a directory containing your builds of both tools.

Corresponding source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.1.tar.xz
License text: https://ffmpeg.org/doxygen/8.1/md_LICENSE.html
Build recipe: https://github.com/Hike-12/Codex_1-Desktop-Wallpapers/blob/v0.3.0/scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh
